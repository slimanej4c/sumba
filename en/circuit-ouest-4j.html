Tour · West Sumba · 4 days / 3 nights

Villages & lagoons of West Sumba

A first tour of the island's West, between traditional villages, a turquoise lagoon and hidden waterfalls.

Home/Tours & Stays/Villages & lagoons of the West

This tour covers the essentials of West Sumba over four days — enough to see the main sites without rushing, with two different places to stay to limit driving time.

Before you read on: the itinerary below sketches out the shape of the trip — the places visited each day, in the most logical order. Exact stops, timings and the precise order of visits are adjusted on the ground based on weather, tides, road conditions and how you're feeling that day. This isn't a minute-by-minute schedule.

Day 1 — Arrival & settling in

Morning: arrival flight into Tambolaka airport, welcomed by your driver.
Afternoon: transfer to Waikabubak (approx. 45 min), with an optional stop along the way if you'd like one — Rumah Budaya Sumba, a small cultural house near the airport, makes for a good break after landing, as does Talasi Estate for a taste of local coffee and cashew milk.
Evening: check in, dinner on your own. A deliberately light first day to recover from the journey.

Day 2 — Ratenggaro & Weekuri Lagoon

Morning: early departure to Kampung Ratenggaro, the traditional village facing the ocean — the morning light here is particularly beautiful.
Midday: a swim at Weekuri Lagoon, a few minutes' drive away; a light lunch on site or at a nearby warung.
Afternoon: back to Waikabubak along the coast, with free stops at whichever beaches catch your eye along the way — the coastal road lends itself well to that.

Day 3 — Weekacura Waterfall & Pantai Marosi

Morning: head out to Weekacura Waterfall, at the end of a rough track — a driver comes along for this day, as the tracks aren't suited to a standard rental vehicle. Swim in the pool if the water level allows.
Lunch: picnic or a stop in a nearby village, depending on the season and what's available.
Afternoon: on to Pantai Marosi — the timing of this stop depends directly on the tide, worth checking the day before so you don't arrive at the wrong moment. A few minutes away, the small cove of Watu Bella offers calmer water if you'd rather end the day with a quiet swim.

Day 4 — Praijing, Tarung & departure

Morning: visit the traditional villages of Praijing and Tarung, both just minutes from Waikabubak — a fitting way to close the trip on local culture rather than on the road.
Midday: back to Tambolaka (45 min), with a comfortable margin before your flight.
Afternoon: transfer to Tambolaka airport and flight home.
