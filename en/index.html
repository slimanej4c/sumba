Sumba isn't a place you rush through. You cross it — one village, one waterfall, one dirt road at a time.

We're based on the island. Not a distant agency running your trip from an office thousands of miles away: here, we know which roads turn bad after rain, which villages ask for a certain kind of respect, and which beaches we still keep to ourselves.

Whether you want to rent a scooter for three days, head out on a day trip into the wild West, or build a full ten-day journey across the island — we adapt to your pace, not the other way around.
