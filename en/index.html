Sumba cannot be visited running. It crosses itself — one village, one waterfall, one dirt road at a time.

We are based on the island. No distant agency managing your stay from an office thousands of kilometers away: here, we know the roads that deteriorate after the rain, the villages that can be visited with respect, and the beaches that we keep to ourselves.

Whether you want to rent a scooter for three days, go on a day trip to the Wild West, or build a complete ten-day trip across the island — we adapt to your pace, not the other way around.
