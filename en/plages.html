The beaches of Sumba

14 beaches, from the west to the southeast of the island — most of them deserted, far from the crowds of Bali.

Home/Discover Sumba/Beaches

Unlike Bali, most beaches in Sumba remain empty even in high season. Here are the 14 we recommend most, organized by area — from the great stone arches of the West to the mangroves of the East.

West

Kampung Ratenggaro

Village facing the ocean, beach at the mouth of the river.

West

Pantai Mandorak

Spectacular natural stone arch.

West

Pantai Marosi

Rock formations, visible at low tide only.

West

Kerewei Beach

Confidential surf spot, beginner area at high tide.

West

Mananga Aba Beach

Deserted beach, off the tourist radar.

West

Pantai Watu Bella

Small cove with crystal clear water, access via Patiala Bala.

West — Kodi

Mbawana Beach

Large stone arch, Kodi sector.

West — Kodi

Pero

Muslim fishing village, colorful boats.

East

Walakiri Beach

The famous “dancing tree” mangroves at sunset.

South-East

Watuparunu Beach

Rock to climb, almost no visitors.

West — Kodi

Tanjung Mareha

Double view of Mbawana and the Maladong cliffs.

West

Pantai Kita

Near Tambolaka, natural pools at low tide.

East

Puru Kambera

Seaside pine trees, wild horses, snorkeling.

South

Tarimbang Beach

Wild bay, famous surf spot.
