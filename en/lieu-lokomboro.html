Lokomboro Waterfall

A secret that even some Waikabubak residents don't know.

Home/Discover Sumba/West/Lokomboro Waterfall

Why this place

There are places that cannot be found in any classic tourist guide. Lokomboro is one of them: a waterfall nestled further north in the West zone, without parking, without signs, without coconut sellers at the entrance. Just the sound of falling water, and the feeling of having got your hands on a real secret.

A little history

With its 30 meters high and its bluish reflections, Lokomboro remains little-known despite a handful of glowing reviews online — a true “hidden gem” in the literal sense. Surprising detail: its watercourse is not only used for tourism, it also supplies a small hydroelectric installation which provides electricity to the surrounding villages, in Pada Ewata.

How to get there

Approximately 1h30 from Tambolaka (37 km), on a partly asphalted road with some damaged sections. Final access requires crossing a bamboo bridge built by locals — it is best to be accompanied by a guide who knows the way, as signage remains minimal.
