Rental quad in Sumba

For trails the scooter doesn't like — hills, dirt roads, access to the most remote waterfalls.

Home/Services/Quad rental

Certain tracks in Sumba are unforgiving to classic scooters: deep ruts after the rain, stony climbs towards the Central waterfalls, laterite paths which turn into a slide from the first downpour. This is exactly the terrain where the quad comes into its own — more ground clearance, more stability, and the assurance of not getting stuck halfway down a waterfall you'll never see.

Quad riding is practiced everywhere in Sumba, not just around a town: outings in the rice terraces of the Center, trails to isolated waterfalls, or savannah crossings in the East — each area of the island has its own terrain suitable for quad biking.

With or without guide

Two possible formulas. Independently, if you are already used to this type of machine: we will entrust you with the quad after a technical check and a few instructions. With a guide, recommended for the less marked trails or for a first experience: he accompanies you in his own vehicle, knows the best passages depending on the weather of the day, and can adjust the itinerary in real time.

Examples of output

Rice terraces around Waikabubak Tracks to the Center waterfalls (Lapopu, Matayangu) Crossing the savannah in the East, towards Wairinding or Puru Kambera Rice fields + waterfall combination in half day

A single collection point, an island to explore

The quad withdraws to Waikabubak, the most central point for traveling to the Central and Western sites where the trails are the most demanding. As for the scooter and the car, delivery to Tambolaka airport is possible on arrival, and collection on site at your hotel at the end of the rental — for an additional fee depending on the distance.

One way between agencies

Are you leaving from Waikabubak but ending your stay at Waingapu? The quad can be picked up in one town and left in another — useful if your route crosses the island from west to east rather than retracing your steps. This service has an additional cost (transportation of the vehicle): request a quote specifying your two cities.

What's included

Helmet and gloves provided, quad checked before each departure (brakes, tires, fuel level at departure). Gasoline consumed during the rental remains at your expense, as with all our rental vehicles.

Good to know

The quad remains a vehicle that requires a minimum of mechanical ease - if this is your first contact with this type of machine, we show you the basics before departure, or we directly recommend the formula with guide. For really technical trails or if you prefer not to drive at all, our private driver service remains a more comfortable alternative.

When a guide accompanies you, he or she can also advise you on good restaurants and warungs near your outing — convenient for combining the excursion with a lunch break that is worth the detour.
