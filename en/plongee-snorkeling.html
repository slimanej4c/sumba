Diving & snorkeling in Sumba

Still confidential in Sumba — organized on request, depending on weather.

Home/Services/Diving & snorkeling

We have to be honest: Sumba is not (yet) an organized diving destination like Bali or the Gili. There is no classic diving club with online reservations and scheduled daily outings - the practice here remains confidential, outside of a center installed in a high-end resort on the island, reserved for its customers.

How do we organize this despite everything

For interested visitors, we can organize a snorkeling or diving trip on request, via our local contacts, depending on the weather, visibility and spots accessible at the time of your stay. The coral areas around Kerewei, in the West, remain the most regularly considered. This is not a fixed product with a fixed price and a guaranteed itinerary — contact us in advance so that we can look together at what is possible during your dates.

Why is it like that

Sumba remains an island focused on waterfalls, villages and savannah rather than the sea as a diving destination — its waters are less documented than those of its Indonesian neighbors. This is also what makes it still virgin territory for those who like to get off the beaten track.
