A tour of the island

The most complete circuit — West, Center, East and South, for those who want to see everything without rushing anything.

Home/Tours & Stays/Tour of the island

The most complete circuit — West, Center, East and South, for those who want to see everything without rushing anything.

Read before continuing: the itinerary below gives the broad outline of the stay - the places visited each day, in the most logical order. The precise stops, times and exact order of visits are adjusted on site according to the weather, tides, road conditions and your current wishes. This is not an hour-by-hour timed program.

Day 1 — Arrival at Tambolaka

Morning: arrival flight, reception.
Afternoon: transfer to Waikabubak.

Day 2 — Ratenggaro & Weekuri

Morning: Ratenggaro.
Afternoon: Weekuri.

Day 3 — Villages of Waikabubak

Morning: Praijing and Tarung.
Afternoon: free time — or an extension to Waikelo Sawah and its rice fields, or to the villages of Praigoli and Waigali.

Day 4 — Anakalang & Lapopu

Morning: Anakalang.
Afternoon: Lapopu.

Day 5 — Transfer to Waingapu

Morning: road to the East (early departure).
Afternoon: installation in Waingapu.

Day 6 — Wairinding & Walakiri

Morning: Wairinding.
Afternoon: Walakiri.

Day 7 — Tanggedu & Puru Kambera

Morning: waterfall of Tanggedu.
Afternoon: savannah of Puru Kambera.

Day 8 — Road to the South

Morning: departure towards Melolo.
Afternoon: installation, free time.

Day 9 — Praiyawang & boat excursion

Morning: village of Praiyawang, with a possible detour towards the preserved beach of Watuparunu.
Afternoon: Sumba crocodile excursion on the mouth of Melolo.

Day 10 — Departure

Morning: return to Waingapu.
Afternoon: transfer to the airport and return flight.

Throughout the stay, your driver-guide advises you on good restaurants and warungs at each stage — don't hesitate to ask him for a recommendation according to your desires of the day.
