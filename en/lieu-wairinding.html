Wairinding Hills

Savannah hills 30 minutes from Waingapu, famous at sunset.

Home/Discover Sumba/East/Wairinding Hills

Why this place

Wairinding is the Sumbanese savannah in its most spectacular form: rolling hills as far as the eye can see, with no trees to break the horizon line. Locals also call it "Sleeping Giant" — seen from the right angle, its silhouette resembles a giant lying on its back. At sunset, the grazing light sculpts the relief in a way that explains why this site comes up in almost all travel journals on Sumba.

A little history

These hills served as the setting for the Indonesian film “The Golden Cane Warrior” (Pendekar Tongkat Emas) — a wink that greatly contributed to their national notoriety. On site, it's hard not to understand why: the undulating relief, without a tree to break the horizon, resembles a life-size movie set.

How to get there

Just 30 minutes east of Waingapu, on a paved road easily accessible by scooter or car.

Associated service

Horseback ride on the beach

Local horses can also be rented here, in a hill version.

What to do there

Climb to the top for the panoramic view (30–45 min walk) Watching the sunset — the most photographed moment Observe the local horses and riders who sometimes cross the hills

Good to know

Plan to arrive late afternoon to have time to ride before sunset, and water — there is no shade on the route.
