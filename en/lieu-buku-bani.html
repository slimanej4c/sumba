Discover Sumba West — Kodi

Kampung Buku Bani

The village where the priests decide the date of Pasola every year — even before announcing it to the other villages.

Home/Discover Sumba/West/Buku Bani

Why this place

Few visitors know it, but Buku Bani occupies a special place in the island's ritual calendar: it is here that the Rato (traditional priests) meet each year to set the date of the Pasola, even before informing the other participating villages such as Kampung Tossi. The village is home to 31 traditional houses (Uma Kalada) and 83 ancient stone tombs, as well as several ancestral objects preserved from generation to generation.

On the eve of Pasola, a series of rituals take place there, including Kawoking — an exchange of songs and traditional verbal jousts — then a ceremony welcoming the Nyale, this marine worm whose appearance on the beaches precedes and announces the ritual combat on horseback.

How to get there

In the Kodi sector, village of Ate Dalo, on the edge of Bondo Kawango beach and its white sand.

What to do there

Discover a village with a unique ritual role on the island Observe traditional houses and stone tombs Combine with Bondo Kawango Beach, right next door

Good to know

If your stay falls in February-March, this village is an interesting starting point to understand how the date of the Pasola is decided before going to the place of the fight itself.
