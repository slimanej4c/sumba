Complete Guide

Discover Sumba,
zone by zone.

Traditional villages, hidden waterfalls, deserted beaches and endless savannahs — organized into four zones to prepare for your exploration. Locally, the island also goes by the name Humba (or Hubba) — literally "the real". Rare geological feature in the archipelago: unlike most Indonesian islands, Sumba is not of volcanic origin - which partly explains its sunshine, among the highest in the entire country.
