Day Trips in Sumba

A vehicle, a guide, several emblematic places in the same area — up to 4 people.

Home/Services/Day trips

For visitors who do not have the time or desire to organize a complete tour, the day trip brings together several emblematic sites of the same area in a single excursion, with a driver who knows the terrain. The price is set per vehicle (up to 4 people), not per person — a pricing method inherited from the island's local operators, not to be confused with multi-day tours billed per person.

Five zones, five different days

Each outing follows the logic of our large zones — West, North (Northside), East, North-East/Savannah and South — presented in detail on our page Discover Sumba. Departure in the morning from your accommodation, return at the end of the afternoon.

Price per vehicle, up to 4 people — not per person. That is approximately $70 / €60 for Westside, and $95 / €85 for Eastside, to be shared between passengers. Gasoline, driver and guide included. Meals and entry to sites not included. Your driver-guide knows the best places in each area — don't hesitate to ask him for a restaurant or warung recommendation for lunch.

Zoom on the North-East / Savanna

Less known than the others, this area concentrates some of the most spectacular savannah landscapes on the island — the waves of golden hills of Puru Kambera, the still confidential waterfall of Waimarang, and the views of Bukit Persaudaraan. An ideal outing for those who have already seen Wairinding and are looking for something just as beautiful, but less crowded.
