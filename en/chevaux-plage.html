Horseback ride on the beach

The horses of Sumba, famous throughout the archipelago, gallop on the sand at sunset.

Home/Services/Horses on the beach

Sumba is renowned throughout Indonesia for its horses - the local breed, called Sandalwood in reference to the sandalwood which has brought the island's commercial wealth for centuries, smaller than Western horses, robust, accustomed for generations to the reliefs of the island. Seeing them gallop across a beach, riding without a saddle, is one of the scenes that comes back most often in memories of travel to Sumba. You will also come across herds of Ongole cattle, another local breed well adapted to the vast savannahs of the island. Horseback riding allows you to experience this scene from the inside rather than looking at it from the sand.

How is it going

The ride takes place on a suitable beach (depending on the area where you are staying), supervised by a local guide who knows the horses and the terrain. No riding experience is necessary — Sumba horses are accustomed to both beginners and experienced riders. Allow around an hour for a standard walk.

The best moment

Late afternoon remains the most sought-after time: the raking light on the sand and the ocean in the background, with the heat fading — both more comfortable for the horses and more spectacular in photos. If your stay falls in July, don't miss the "1001 Sandalwood Horses" festival - eight days of celebrations across several towns on the island, where the major ikat weaving festival also takes place, bringing together more than 2000 artisans. The same period also hosts horse races in the East, where young riders compete for victory until August and October.

Good to know

This activity must be booked in advance, ideally through your accommodation or directly with us, depending on the area. The guide can also advise you on the best spots in the region to extend your day.
