Boat — river & wildlife observation, Sumba

Mouth of Melolo, southeast of Sumba — at a respectful distance.

Home/Services/River boat

This is undoubtedly the most unusual activity offered in Sumba: a boat trip to the mangrove and river mouth areas around Melolo, in the south-east of the island - where the river meets the ocean in a landscape of submerged roots and mud banks. No other operator on the island offers this type of outing as such.

Sumba crocodile excursion — a population, at a good distance

It must be said clearly: the mouth areas around Melolo — particularly that of the Kayouri River — are home to a population of marine crocodiles, known and documented locally. This is neither a legend nor a marketing argument - it is a reality which imposes simple rules: outing exclusively by boat (never on foot or swimming in these areas), always with a local guide who knows the areas of usual presence, and a constant safety distance maintained with the animal if there is an observation. This is not an "approachable" or thrill-seeking activity: the objective is to observe an intact mangrove ecosystem, the wildlife found there—crocodiles included, from a distance—and a landscape that most visitors to the island never see.

What you actually see

The boat progresses gently along the mangrove channels, between aerial roots and sandbanks, until the mouth opens onto the ocean. Water birds, sometimes a few dolphins offshore depending on the season, and the particular silence of this area still preserved from tourism.

Terms

This activity is highly weather and tide dependent — it may be canceled or postponed if conditions are unsafe. Limited number of participants per boat; life jackets provided and obligatory.

Your guide, also good advice

The guide who accompanies you on this excursion also knows the good addresses in the Melolo region - restaurants and warungs where to extend the day after the boat trip. Don't hesitate to ask him for a recommendation before leaving.
