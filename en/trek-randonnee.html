Trek & hiking in Sumba

Isolated villages and waterfalls that can only be reached on foot.

Home/Services/Trek & hiking

Some of the most beautiful places in Sumba cannot be approached by vehicle. The last portion is necessarily done on foot — sometimes a few hundred meters from an improvised parking lot, sometimes several kilometers of trail through the savannah or forest. Our accompanied trek service covers these last portions, with a local guide who knows the terrain and, often, the families of the villages crossed.

Where we walk in Sumba

The Center and the East concentrate the majority of interesting trails: access to certain waterfalls still without a road (Weekacura, Lokomboro), crossing the savannah around Wairinding, or walk to traditional villages that no vehicle track serves. For more experienced hikers, the Manupeu Tanah Daru and Laiwangi Wanggameti national parks, in the heart of the island, protect still preserved species - birds, wild boars, monkeys and a wide diversity of butterflies - on less marked trails, to be organized with a local guide. For the most daring, the Kanabu Wai waterfall remains almost mythical: it can only be earned at the cost of an expedition of around 12 hours of walking from the nearest village, with a night in the middle of the jungle — an adventure in its own right, reserved for a handful of hikers each year.

Level & duration

Most of our treks remain accessible to anyone in normal shape — between 45 minutes and 3 hours of walking, without extreme elevation gain. We adapt the route to your pace and the weather of the day, the heat in the middle of the day can make certain trails more demanding.

Your guide, also good advice

The guide who accompanies you on the trail also knows the good restaurants and warungs of the nearest village - useful for extending the outing with a lunch that is worth the detour.
