The great crossing

The island from one end to the other — from Tambolaka to Waingapu, without going back to square one.

Home/Tours & Stays/The great crossing

The island from one end to the other — from Tambolaka to Waingapu, without going back to square one.

Read before continuing: the itinerary below gives the broad outline of the stay - the places visited each day, in the most logical order. The precise stops, times and exact order of visits are adjusted on site according to the weather, tides, road conditions and your current wishes. This is not an hour-by-hour timed program.

Day 1 — Arrival at Tambolaka

Morning: arrival flight, reception.
Afternoon: transfer to Waikabubak.

Day 2 — Ratenggaro & Weekuri

Morning: Ratenggaro.
Afternoon: Weekuri.

Day 3 — Villages & Lapopu waterfall

Morning: Praijing and Tarung.
Afternoon: waterfall of Lapopu.

Day 4 — Anakalang Megaliths

Morning: Anakalang.
Afternoon: transfer route to Waingapu (early departure recommended).

Day 5 — Settling in Waingapu

Morning: arrival at Waingapu, installation.
Afternoon: free time, local market — or a short outing to Bukit Tenau, 6 km from the city center.

Day 6 — Wairinding & Walakiri

Morning: hills of Wairinding.
Afternoon: sunset at Walakiri.

Day 7 — Tanggedu Waterfall

Morning: Tanggedu.
Afternoon: return to Waingapu.

Day 8 — Departure

Morning: Free morning.
Afternoon: transfer to Waingapu airport and return flight.

Throughout the stay, your driver-guide advises you on good restaurants and warungs at each stage — don't hesitate to ask him for a recommendation according to your desires of the day.
