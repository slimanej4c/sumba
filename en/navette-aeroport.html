A la carte services · Sumba

Shuttle airport ↔ hotel — Sumba

Available 24/7 at both airports on the island.

Home/Services/Airport shuttle

A driver will wait for you at the terminal exit, whatever time of your flight.

Sumba has two airports — Tambolaka in the west and Waingapu in the east — and depending on your accommodation, the journey from the airstrip to your room can take anywhere from 20 minutes to over 2 hours.

The service is available 24/7, including night flights and late connections — flight times to Sumba change often, your driver follows actual schedules rather than a fixed schedule.
