Kerewei Beach

A still confidential beach, renowned among surfers for its long, regular straights.

Home/Discover Sumba/West/Kerewei Beach

Why this place

Nestled in the southwest corner of the island, just after Marosi, Kerewei remains under the radar despite a very good wave - white sand, coconut trees, relaxed local atmosphere. A small, gentler portion of the bay is also suitable for swimming and learning to surf at high tide.

How to get there

Approximately 1 hour drive from Waikabubak, or 2 hours from Tambolaka airport. The last portion is on a single track.

What to do there

Surfing for intermediate to advanced levels (reef break, long straights) Softer area for beginners at high tide Relaxation on a beach that is still very uncrowded Possible combination with Marosi, a few minutes away

Good to know

The bottom is coral — bring water shoes. Surfing equipment remains limited on site; bring your own board if you are demanding.
