scooter rental in Sumba

A scooter in good condition, two helmets, a clear deposit. Choose your pickup city.

Home/Services/Scooter rental

The easiest way to visit the island at your own pace, without relying on a driver.

Our scooters are checked before each rental: brakes, tires, lighting. You leave with two helmets, a full tank of fuel to be filled, and a deposit which is returned to you in full upon return if all goes well.

The price drops from the second day of rental — the longer you keep the scooter, the lower the price per day.

One way between cities: Pick up your scooter in Tambolaka and return it to Waingapu, or the other way around — handy if your route crosses the island without making a return trip. A conveyance surcharge applies depending on the distance. On the island, rental is most often done between individuals, and you then have to return the scooter to the initial starting point — this is a point on which we voluntarily distinguish ourselves. Delivery to the airport also possible on arrival, with collection at the same location on your departure.
