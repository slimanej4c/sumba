Private driver for the day — Sumba

For roads that scooters and self-drive cars don't like.

Home/Services/Private driver

Certain sites in Sumba are worth it: rutted tracks after the rain, fording, total absence of signage. On these roads, a driver who knows the way makes all the difference between a great day and an afternoon spent turning around. This is particularly true for the most remote waterfalls in the West and Center, or for reaching certain traditional villages in a single day without wasting time getting your bearings.

What's included

The driver accompanies you all day, knows the shortcuts, knows where to park near the villages, and can often interpret for the locals for the usual formalities (payment of the passage, purchase of a coconut as a thank you, etc.). The vehicle and gasoline are included in the package.

For what types of outputs

The private driver is particularly recommended for: difficult-to-access waterfalls (Weekacura, Lokomboro), circuits combining several areas in the same day, families with children, or simply if you prefer to enjoy the landscape rather than concentrate on the road.

How is it going

The driver will meet you directly at your accommodation, with an itinerary already discussed in advance — you can also remain flexible and decide on stops throughout the day. There is no mileage limit for a standard day, within a reasonable time limit.

A real guide, not just a driver

Beyond the road, your driver knows the good local addresses - restaurants and warungs where to eat well at each stop, the best times to avoid crowds at a site, or an activity that was not initially planned but which is worth the detour that day. Do not hesitate to ask for advice: it is often the best source of information on site, well ahead of any paper guide.
