Car rental · Tambolaka, Sumba

Car in Tambolaka, Sumba

Home/Car rental/Tambolaka

From Tambolaka airport, a car allows you to comfortably reach the sites of the West with family or several people, without depending on the limited space of a scooter.

One way possible

You can collect your car in Tambolaka and leave it in another town on the island — Waikabubak or Waingapu — if your itinerary does not return here at the end of your stay. A conveyance surcharge applies, to be confirmed depending on the exact distance.

Airport delivery & collection

On request, we deliver the vehicle directly to the airport upon your arrival, and pick it up from the same location before your return flight — no need to contact an agency in town.

Prices

Prices on quote depending on duration, formula (self-drive or with driver) and desired vehicle model.

Available models

Compact SUVs like Toyota Rush or Daihatsu Terios for small groups, or minivans like Toyota Avanza/Xenia for families or groups of up to 6 people. All our vehicles are checked before each departure - brakes, tires, air conditioning - essential under the Western sun.

Drive from Tambolaka

The coastal road to Ratenggaro and Weekuri is generally in good condition, passable without difficulty by self-drive. On the other hand, the trails towards the more isolated waterfalls (Weekacura, Lokomboro) are not suitable for a standard vehicle — the chauffeured option remains preferable for these excursions.
