Discover Sumba West — Kodi

Mbawana Beach

A large, spectacular stone arch, on a coast still largely untouched by tourism.

Home/Discover Sumba/West/Mbawana Beach

Why this place

Mbawana is distinguished by its large stone arch which frames the horizon. In the Kodi district, in the far west of the island, it remains largely untouched by tourism despite its beauty: massive cliffs, spectacular rock formations, and that special golden light at sunset.

How to get there

Approximately 1h30 to 2h drive from Tambolaka (~56 km), on roads mixing tarmac sections and rougher tracks at the end. Access to the beach itself is via a fairly steep natural staircase, cut into the cliff - bring good walking shoes, the round trip requires a minimum of physical fitness.

What to do there

Photograph the stone arch and surrounding cliffs Sunset, the most spectacular moment on site Combine with the nearby Tanjung Mareha viewpoint

Good to know

No infrastructure on site. A small contribution is generally required at the entrance (around 50,000 IDR), and local children sometimes offer to guide the descent for a tip – negotiate the amount before leaving rather than on the way back. Best period: May to October, when the roads are most passable.
