Tour · South — East · 7 days / 6 nights

Wild coasts of the South

The least traveled area of the island — weaver villages, wild river mouths and deserted beaches.

Home/Tours & Stays/Wild Southern Coasts

The least traveled area of the island — weaver villages, wild river mouths and deserted beaches.

Read before continuing: the itinerary below gives the broad outline of the stay - the places visited each day, in the most logical order. The precise stops, times and exact order of visits are adjusted on site according to the weather, tides, road conditions and your current wishes. This is not an hour-by-hour timed program.

Day 1 — Arrival at Waingapu

Morning: arrival flight, reception.
Afternoon: installation in Waingapu.

Day 2 — Wairinding & Walakiri

Morning: Wairinding.
Afternoon: Walakiri at sunset.

Day 3 — Road to the South

Morning: early departure to Melolo (several hours drive).
Afternoon: installation, free time.

Day 4 — Kampung Adat Praiyawang

Morning: village of Praiyawang, royal tombs and ikat weaving.
Afternoon: free time, purchasing weaving directly from the artisans — or an outing to the still confidential beach of Watuparunu, nearby.

Day 5 — Sumba crocodile excursion

Morning: exit in boat on the mouth of Melolo, observation of the fauna.
Afternoon: return, free time.

Day 6 — Tarimbang Beach

Morning: road to Tarimbang.
Afternoon: afternoon on the beach, return to Waingapu at the end of the day.

Day 7 — Departure

Morning: Free morning.
Afternoon: transfer to Waingapu airport and return flight.

Throughout the stay, your driver-guide advises you on good restaurants and warungs at each stage — don't hesitate to ask him for a recommendation according to your desires of the day.
