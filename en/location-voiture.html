Rental car in Sumba

With or without driver, in the three main towns of the island.

Home/Services/Car rental

For a group, a family with children, or simply if you prefer the comfort of a closed cabin for long distances between the sites of Sumba, the car remains the most versatile option. Two possible options: self-drive (you drive) for the main roads, or with driver for more difficult tracks and to travel without worrying about driving on the left... or rather on the right, but on sometimes surprising roads.

Three withdrawal cities

As with the scooter, you can collect your vehicle in Tambolaka, Waikabubak or Waingapu. Each city has its own page with practical details and proximity to the corresponding airport.

One way between agencies

If your trip crosses the island — for example departing from Tambolaka in the west and returning from Waingapu in the east — you do not have to retrace your steps to return the vehicle. It is possible to pick it up in one city and leave it in another, for a delivery charge to be confirmed depending on the distance between the two points.

Airport delivery & collection

On arrival or departure, we can deliver the vehicle directly to Tambolaka or Waingapu airport, and collect it from the same location at the end of your stay - practical if you do not want to waste time reaching an agency in town as soon as you get off the plane.

With driver: a guide, not just a driver

If you opt for the chauffeur-driven option, he also advises you on good restaurants and warungs at each stage of your journey — a valuable source of local information, in addition to driving.
